## About This Pack
**Ender Vantasy** is a custom resource pack designed specifically for RPG adventures and high-performance PvP/PvE combat in Minecraft Java Edition. All world building blocks, ambient environmental sounds, and passive mob textures remain **100% vanilla**, maintaining original game aesthetics and optimal FPS.

---

## Features Overview

### 1. Weapons and Combat Gear
- **Swords and Melee Weapons**: Custom textures across all material tiers (Netherite, Diamond, Iron, etc.).
- **Bows and Crossbows**: Precision multi-stage pulling animations.
- **Tridents**: Custom in-hand 3D models, projectile textures, and dynamic Riptide animations.
- **Shields**: Clean shield models with full support for custom banner patterns.
- **Arrows**: Enhanced projectile models for Regular, Spectral, and Tipped Arrows.

### 2. Armor Sets and Smithing Trims
- **Complete Armor Sets**: Custom inventory icons and 3D worn models (Netherite, Diamond, Iron, Gold, Chainmail, Leather).
- **Modern Equipment Layers**: Fully compatible with Minecraft 1.21.2+ equipment components.
- **Armor Trims**: Seamless support for all smithing template patterns (Vex, Ward, Silence, Tide, etc.).

### 3. Totem of Undying and Utility
- **Totem of Undying**: Distinctive totem item sprites and dynamic activation particles.

### 4. Combat Visual Effects
- **Critical Hits**: Sharp combat strike particles.
- **Sword Sweep**: Dynamic horizontal attack sweep animation.
- **Enchanted Glint**: Subtle magic shimmer that preserves item detail.
- **Emissive Textures**: Glow map support via OptiFine and Iris Shaders (`emissive.properties`).

### 5. Custom Item Textures (CIT)
- Rename items in an Anvil to transform weapons and gear into custom RPG equipment.
- Custom crowns and diadems for renamed helmets.
- Themed enchanted book covers by enchantment type.
- Custom Axolotl and Tropical Fish bucket textures.

---

## Vanilla Defaults
To preserve competitive fairness, visual clarity, and high performance:
- **Elytra Wings**: Uses 100% vanilla flight models and inventory icons.
- **Turtle Shell**: Exempt from custom helmet overrides to preserve instant visual identification.
- **Building Blocks and Terrain**: Stone, dirt, wood, foliage, and sky remain completely vanilla.
- **Potions**: Kept strictly vanilla for clean combat visibility.
- **Main Menu**: Retains the standard Minecraft title screen.

---

## Version Compatibility
- **Minecraft Java Edition**: Compatible with versions **1.20 through 1.21.x** (supports pack formats 16 through 76 with automatic component overlays).
- **Recommended Mods**:
  - **OptiFine**, OR
  - **Fabric / Iris Shaders** with [CIT Resewn](https://modrinth.com/mod/cit-resewn) and [Entity Model Features](https://modrinth.com/mod/entity-model-features) for full CIT and emissive texture support.

---

## Installation Guide
1. Download the latest `Ender Vantasy.zip` from the [Releases](https://github.com/NDERXZ/Ender-Vantasy-Custom-Texturepack/releases) page.
2. Launch Minecraft -> Navigate to **Options...** -> **Resource Packs...**.
3. Click **Open Pack Folder**.
4. Place `Ender Vantasy.zip` into the folder.
5. Move the pack to the right-hand list (**Selected**), then click **Done**.
